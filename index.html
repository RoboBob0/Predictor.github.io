The outer bezel (1–37) is fixed — just a scale. Enter a number and the inner table rotates on its own to bring that number to the pointer. You can still drag the table by hand any time.
Enter one or more bezel positions (1–37), comma-separated. For each, it finds whatever wheel number currently sits at that same physical spot and lights all of them up in green — on the wheel and the felt table. It never highlights the typed number directly.
